No. Our planned product is a personalized digital PDF. We do not currently offer physical printing or shipping.

Can I order today?

Not yet. Our online ordering and payment system is being prepared. This site explains the planned service but does not take payments or collect personal photos.

When will I receive my magazine?

We will publish our delivery timeframe before taking any orders. A private proof-and-approval process is planned before the final PDF is delivered.

Once ordering opens, uploads will be handled in a private customer workspace. We plan to remove source photos and working files within 24 hours of final delivery, and provide the finished PDF for a limited download window. The final policy will be disclosed before purchase.

What if I need a refund?

No transactions are accepted yet. Before ordering opens, we will publish and disclose the applicable cancellation, refund, and dispute process. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
