# Bilibili Channel Editorial Strategy v0.3 — G3R PASS

## 1. Channel Positioning

Primary platform: **Bilibili**  
Editorial cadence: **DAILY target**  
Decision status: **G3R PASS**  
Primary objective: **Audience growth → repeated viewing → trust → future monetization**

Canonical positioning:

> **一个固定 IP 带观众进入 AI 时代正在发生的故事；故事本身值得看，AI 机制负责让观众看懂世界，方法只在问题天然需要时出现。**

Not:
- AI news reader;
- encyclopedia-style AI explainer;
- story skin wrapped around definitions.

## 2. Core Content Formula

```text
Human situation / abnormal event
→ IP enters the problem
→ desire / action
→ expectation-result gap
→ complications
→ discovery / turning point
→ AI mechanism becomes visible
→ one of two payoffs:
   A. Mental-model payoff
   B. Action payoff
→ story closes
```

The technical term may appear early when it is itself the current search/hot signal, but **definition must not replace the story**.

## 3. Business Job vs Editorial Form

These are separate axes.

### Business Job
- DISCOVERY
- TRUST
- SOLUTION

### Editorial Mode

#### STORY_MODEL
Story → mechanism → mental model / judgment.

Use when:
- the main value is understanding;
- advice would be forced or unstable;
- the mechanism itself changes how the audience sees AI.

#### STORY_ACTION
Story → mechanism → one practical decision framework / behavior.

Use when:
- the viewer can do something useful immediately;
- the action follows directly from the mechanism;
- practical value strengthens trust and saves/favorites.


## 4. Initial Content Portfolio — Season 0 Hypothesis

For the first 21 published Bilibili episodes, use this as an **experimental distribution**, not a permanent quota:

- 5 / 7 days: STORY_MODEL
- 2 / 7 days: STORY_ACTION

Equivalent approximation:
- ~70% story + concept/mental model
- ~30% story + actionable method

Hot topics may replace a planned slot but inherit the best-fit editorial mode.

Why:
- a new account first needs discovery and a recognizable creative identity;
- practical/actionable episodes create saves, search value and trust;
- actionable episodes add practical value without turning the channel into a tool manual.

## 6. Bilibili-first Duration

Do not use 70–85 seconds as the Bilibili canonical duration.

Initial duration bands:

- HOT / timely story: **3–5 min**
- standard story episode: **3–5 min**
- exceptional story: may exceed 5 min when the narrative genuinely requires it

Duration is a result of narrative requirements, not a quota.

The previous 70–85s G3 samples remain:
`SHORT_FORM_WRITER_VALIDATION_REFERENCE_ONLY`.

## 7. Why Bilibili Supports This Direction

Latest retrievable Bilibili official investor materials used in this review report:
- watch time for videos over five minutes grew about 20% YoY in Q3 2025;
- Bilibili explicitly describes mid-to-long-form videos as a hallmark and says creator-led storytelling keeps viewers engaged;
- AI-related content remained one of the fastest-growing categories;
- official creator data center exposes average watch duration, retention curve, play-to-follow rate, and audience/fan interest data.

Therefore we optimize against **actual watch duration and retention**, not an assumed short-video duration formula.

## 8. IP / Narrative Point of View

### Default
**First-person channel IP**.

The IP is the recurring doorway into the world.

Preferred:
- “我最近干了件挺蠢的事……”
- “我本来以为……结果……”
- “前两天我让我的 AI 搭档……”

### Important trust boundary
The IP may be a clearly fictional/stylized protagonist in the channel world.

Do not fabricate real-world personal experience:
- no fake “I tested this product yesterday”;
- no fake “I personally paid / lost money / worked at company X”;
unless it actually happened or is clearly framed as fictional simulation.

### POV routing
- first person: default, IP as protagonist / witness;
- third person: real external case or supporting characters;
- second person: sparingly, to invite mental simulation or return the question to the viewer.

Canonical pattern:

```text
I experience
→ world / other characters act
→ you are invited to recognize the same problem
```

## 9. Fixed IP, Semi-fixed World

Reuse:
- protagonist IP;
- recurring AI partner;
- recurring friend/colleague/manager/customer archetypes;
- canonical visual style;
- a small set of reusable locations.

Do not force every topic into one literal island or office.

The “Little Island Economics” lesson we retain is:

> **Mechanism should operate as a world rule and produce consequences through character actions.**

Not:
> every topic must use the same metaphorical world.

## 10. Story Quality Rule for Bilibili

Longer duration does not mean “add more explanation.”

A 3–5 minute standard story needs:
- at least one clear desire;
- at least one meaningful first action;
- multiple *escalating* complications;
- at least one false solution / misjudgment when natural;
- a real turning point;
- mechanism revealed through consequences;
- a payoff that changes what the protagonist/viewer understands or does.

Forbidden long-form failure:

```text
2 min story
+ 3 min lecture
```

Preferred:

```text
story beat
→ consequence
→ new attempt
→ new complication
→ partial insight
→ action
→ mechanism becomes undeniable
→ payoff
```

## 11. Actionability Gate

After KnowledgeCore and StoryPremise, ask:

1. Does this mechanism imply a useful viewer action?
2. Is the action stable and evidence-based?
3. Can it be explained without adding a second unrelated mechanism?
4. Does the action naturally resolve or extend the story problem?

If YES → STORY_ACTION candidate.

If NO → finish with a strong mental model. Do not manufacture a tutorial.

Examples:

- Memory / Context:
  action can be “what belongs in current context vs persistent memory”.
- Agent:
  action can be “where approval checkpoints belong”.
- MCP:
  for a general audience, mental-model payoff may be stronger than forcing an implementation method.

## 16. Opinion / Viewpoint Policy

观点不是第三种内容模式，也不是每期必需，但它可以成为一篇故事的**中心意义**。

关键不是“少表达观点”，而是：

> **观点可以是故事的灵魂，但不能是作者的演讲。**

### Internal order

Before writing a thesis, define a **Controlling Question**.

Then, when a viewpoint exists:

```text
Fact / event
→ mechanism
→ Idea vs Counter-Idea
→ story consequence
→ climax / meaningful choice proves the meaning
→ narrator may lightly name it
```

### Allowed viewpoint

A useful viewpoint:
- is rooted in locked facts / KnowledgeCore;
- answers a human question created by the AI mechanism;
- has a real counter-position inside the story;
- is narrower than a universal social prediction;
- is tested by character action and consequence;
- preferably becomes clear through the climax before the narrator names it.

### High-risk viewpoint

Return or soften when:
- thesis is decided first and story is selected only to prove it;
- one anecdote is generalized to everyone / the whole industry / the future;
- motives are inferred without evidence;
- the narrator repeatedly states the thesis instead of dramatizing it;
- the story collapses unless viewers already agree with the opinion.

Default budget:
- 0 or 1 primary viewpoint per episode;
- no forced “升华”;
- if the climax already proves the meaning, shorten the explicit explanation.

Failure:
`RETURN_OPINION_OVERREACH`.

## 17. Story Beat Quality Contract

A story segment is good only when it changes the state of the story.

Every meaningful beat should contain most of:

```text
Immediate want
→ action
→ resistance / response
→ result
→ state change
→ next question / next decision
```

A state change means at least one meaningful variable changed:
- knowledge;
- goal;
- strategy;
- stakes;
- relationship;
- emotion that changes behavior;
- available options.

Changing only location, wording, or visual composition does not count.

### Beat PASS test

After removing the beat, ask:
1. Does the next beat still make equal sense?
2. Did the protagonist learn / lose / gain / decide nothing?
3. Did the situation fail to become harder, clearer, stranger or more consequential?

If yes, the beat is probably removable.

Canonical definition:

> **好故事不是“发生很多事”，而是每件事都导致下一件事，而且人物经过它以后已经和进去之前不一样。**
