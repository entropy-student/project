## Main weakness

The metaphor is doing a lot of work.
Unlike Agent, the plot is less externally consequential and more conceptual.

Risk:
> if visuals are weak, “桌子/纸/笔记” can become a prolonged analogy rather than a living story.

Mitigation for later Director stage:
- make the narrator's behavior visually escalate;
- reuse the “办公室扩建” joke;
- avoid diagram-style context-window visuals until the late reveal.
