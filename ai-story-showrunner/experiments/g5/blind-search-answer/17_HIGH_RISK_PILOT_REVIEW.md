Repeated additions included bubbles, arrows, checklists, summary slogans, explanatory titles and duplicated before/after protagonists.
Conclusion: unspecified explanatory graphics default to `DO_NOT_ADD`.

### DERIVE_EDIT source incompatibility
Caught in VB009, VB012 and VB044.
Rule validated: material POV / subject-set / crop-role / primary UI-geometry changes require fresh GENERATE unless a compatible accepted source exists.

### Character identity drift
Observed juvenile/cute reinterpretation, enlarged eyes, rounder face, shorter chin and hoodie-like costume drift.
Prompt-level identity locks reduced drift but did not eliminate it.
Conclusion: real canonical image-reference binding is mandatory before deterministic production.

### Brand / UI expansion
Generation sometimes invented brands/logos, broad browser chrome, full help-center layouts or excessive decorative context.
Conclusion: brand NONE + low-density crop constraints remain required; exact critical text stays POST_OVERLAY.
