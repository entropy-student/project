## Reject summary

The most common reasons visually impressive work failed the Core-Aha bar:

- **Page flip first:** communicates “flipbook widget,” not “your photo became a personalized magazine.”
- **Hover/cursor first:** poor mobile discoverability and unnecessary input dependency.
- **Pixel/WebGL distortion:** decorative spectacle can overpower premium editorial/product meaning and increases fallback cost.
- **Infinite/scroll-jacked galleries:** delay the moment of understanding and fight normal page behavior.
- **Before/after sliders:** imply comparison rather than transformation into a gift.
- **Generic gallery/lightbox:** enlarges a photo but does not change its identity.
- **Platform-heavy templates:** homepage is already frozen; importing Framer/Webflow/React architecture would solve the wrong problem.
- **Unclear/restrictive license:** retained only as reference; not copied.
