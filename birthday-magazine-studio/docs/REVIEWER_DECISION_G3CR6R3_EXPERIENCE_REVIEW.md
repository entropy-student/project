## Why final-product proof matters

The repo already contains a real 12-page G2BR3 rendered magazine and contact sheet. The customer-facing site should use this evidence.

Showing only stylized covers/spreads or generated marketing art creates a Trust gap:
- user cannot see what the actual 12-page purchase looks like;
- marketing visuals can outrun the deterministic deliverable;
- purchase desire is harder to build when the finished object is abstract.

## Why motion belongs in this correction

Motion is treated as a **WEBSITE Creative / Value Presentation** lever, not as a property of the magazine deliverable and not as evidence by itself.

The birthday magazine remains a static 12-page PDF. The website may animate its interface, sections, decorative layers and state transitions; it must not imply that the purchased magazine itself is animated.

It should help:
- direct attention;
- create editorial depth;
- demonstrate the magazine object;
- make sample pages feel tactile;
- make personalization feel intentional.

It should not become a distracting feature project.

## Stop rule after this correction

After G3CR6R3:

- if Reviewer and Owner accept the experience, freeze the frontend;
- do not continue broad aesthetic iteration without new user evidence;
- proceed toward governed public/real-user validation readiness.

Real market behavior remains unproven.
