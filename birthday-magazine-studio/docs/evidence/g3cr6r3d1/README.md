## Interpretation limits

Full-page captures do not trigger every scroll-dependent state simultaneously and omit some fixed backgrounds/cards. Use section and motion frames to judge those regions. Reduced-motion screenshots are timed samples, not compliance proof. Mobile uses Chromium viewport emulation, not a physical phone; artificial hover is not touch behavior. Original easing/loop periods, Webflow source/library and additional home variants were not extracted or inferred. Screenshot files are reference evidence only; do not ship their proprietary imagery, logos, or text as project media.
