## Research reset

Research restarts independently for two surfaces:

1. **CORE_AHA_INTERACTION**
   - must be directly viewable;
   - description must match the actual live interaction;
   - should communicate photo -> personalized magazine/product transformation rather than decorative motion;
   - mobile/reduced-motion feasibility and source/license status must be recorded.

2. **MAGAZINE_P1_P12_VISUAL_SYSTEM**
   - seek complete editorial/photo-book/magazine layout families, not isolated pretty pages;
   - cover P1-P12 roles coherently;
   - prioritize systems that can be independently implemented in the existing HTML/CSS/PDF architecture;
   - record source/license/asset/font boundaries separately from visual quality.

The prior 91-candidate package remains useful evidence and reject history, but its shortlist and saturation finding do not close these two surfaces after this Owner reset.
