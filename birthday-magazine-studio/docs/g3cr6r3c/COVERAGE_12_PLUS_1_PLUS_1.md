# 12 + 1 + 1 Coverage Map

| Surface | Frozen purpose | Candidate references | Transferable lesson | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Cover | Identity/first impact | C03, C04, C28, C61, C76 | Cover hierarchy, portrait crop, issue identity, controlled opening | No copied art, marks or personal facts |
| P2 Opening Note | Warm entry | C11, C16, C47, C81 | Short copy/image rhythm and narrative beats | Avoid long-form scale/media dependence |
| P3 Who They Are | Recipient profile | C03, C71, C77, C84 | Portrait and profile hierarchy | Intake-grounded facts only |
| P4-P5 Feature Memory | Two-page feature | C04, C11, C70, C85, C69 | Paired image/text pacing, chapter arc | No commissioned asset or hosted-media dependency |
| P6 Dynamic A | Supported module | C61, C81-C91 | Module-specific visual grammar | Only when intake supports it |
| P7 Why They Matter | Human significance | C70, C77, C82 | Compact story and portrait/proof-point rhythm | No fake testimonial or unsupported claim |
| P8-P9 Photo Story/Current Era | Photo sequence | C15, C79, C86, C91, C20-C27 | Progressive reveal, crop and sequence rhythm | No external image POST, video/WebGL prerequisite or inaccessible gesture |
| P10 Dynamic B | Distinct second module | C61, C81-C91 | Separate grammar from Module A | Never fill page without evidence |
| P11 Birthday Letter | Closing note | C11, C16, C78, C81 | Calm typography and paced ending | Do not copy prose |
| P12 Back Cover | Close/colophon | C03, C04, C61, C76 | Minimal issue marker and final image | No invented imprint/credits |
| Homepage | Gift conversion path | C44, C61, C70, C79, C15 | Cover-led first view, selected samples, clear preview CTA | Not SaaS, author portfolio or alternate cart |
| Core interaction | Local photo to magazine aha | C28, C31, C32, C39/C42, C51, C61, C63 | Stage a local photo into a cover then one spread reveal | No server/third-party upload or model call |

The 12 pages remain one deterministic architecture; Homepage and interaction are additional surfaces, not extra pages or a parallel editor. A reduced-motion-aware CSS transform is a lower-cost hypothesis than WebGL; this research Gate does not choose or implement it.
