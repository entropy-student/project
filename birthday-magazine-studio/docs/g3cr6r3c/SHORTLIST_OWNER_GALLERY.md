## Owner viewing order

1. Marginalia (S, 86): use the marketplace page to open the live preview; inspect the issue cover, marginalia, sticky contents, back-issue ring, paper/ink control and stated phone/reduced-motion intent. Its free remix is marked Limited; clarify rights before copying anything.
2. Preview to Full Content Page Transition (A): inspect a preview card expanding into editorial content; tutorial reuse rights are unverified.
3. GulLabs Flipbook (A): inspect the tactile reader and repository license boundary. It is a reader, not the deterministic PDF renderer.
4. NOWNESS (A): inspect cinematic image staging; its video stack is not a local-photo preview implementation.
5. SNV Annual Report 2025 (A): inspect portrait, chapter, metric and message sequencing.
6. Emergence Magazine (A): inspect photo-led narrative pacing.
7. The Gourmand (A): inspect cover typography and photographic art direction.
8. Poetry in Motion (A): inspect modular narrative and listen/watch branches; its media is not reusable.
9. Astro Kami (A): inspect MIT source and live demo; a WordPress/Gutenberg port is still required.
10. Cover Page Transition (A): inspect the opening reference; do not copy unlicensed tutorial code.
11. The Gentlewoman (A): inspect portrait covers and issue/archive coherence.
12. PIN–UP (A): inspect cover/portrait direction.
13. The Bitter Southerner (A): inspect human-scale story tone.
14. The Pudding (A): inspect the single-interaction visual essay; project licenses vary.
