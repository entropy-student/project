Sunday Tomato Sauce

From the sample recipe card

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1.5 kg ripe tomatoes

Method

  1. Warm the oil and stir in the paste.
  2. Simmer for 45 min, then season to taste.
