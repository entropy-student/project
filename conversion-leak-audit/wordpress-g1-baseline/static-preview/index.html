Evidence before advice

A public scan should say what it observed, what may matter, and what still needs store data. It should not invent a revenue root cause.

1. ObserveCheck bounded public facts: purchase actions, mobile blockers, pricing consistency, policies and subscription transparency.
2. PrioritizeApply store-type and context gates so irrelevant rules stay silent.
3. Fix or verifyGive a concrete fix or identify the next evidence needed instead of guessing.
