CORE-007 — Product price not visible near purchase action

Observed fact: A direct-purchase product page exposes a purchase action, but no visible product price is present near the purchase decision.

Source / evidence: /products/example — visible page content near the purchase action.

Why it may matter: Shoppers may need clear price information before they can evaluate the purchase.

First move: Make the current purchase price visible near the primary purchase action.

Limitation: This synthetic observation does not establish lost sales or prove that price visibility changed a purchase decision.

PHYS-002 — Shipping information is hard to find

Observed fact: Shipping cost or delivery timing was not found in the expected purchase-adjacent pages checked by the demo fixture.

Source / evidence: /products/example and /cart.

Why it may matter: Missing or distant shipping information can leave an important purchase question unresolved.

First move: Surface shipping cost and timing closer to the product or cart decision point.

Limitation: The scan did not find this information in the checked pages; that does not prove it is absent elsewhere or caused a shopper to leave.

PHYS-001 — Return information is hard to find

Observed fact: No clear return or refund policy link or return information was found in the checked public navigation and purchase context.

Source / evidence: /, /products/example, and /faq.

Why it may matter: Return terms are a common purchase-risk question and should be easy to discover before checkout.

First move: Make return and refund terms directly discoverable from the relevant purchase journey.

Limitation: This finding describes only the pages checked in the synthetic fixture; it does not establish a cause of lost sales.
