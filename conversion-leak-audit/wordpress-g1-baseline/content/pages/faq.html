FAQ

Can a public scan explain why sales are low?

No. A public scan can identify observable defects and research-supported risks. Traffic quality, funnel drop-off and causal impact require store data or experiments.

Will you log into my store?

No. The scanner is intentionally limited to publicly available pages and does not access your store account or admin area.

Do you submit checkout or payment forms?

No. The scanner does not complete purchases or payments.

What happens when a site blocks the scan?

The report marks the audit as incomplete. It must not convert “not observed” into “missing from the website.”
