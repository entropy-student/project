Evidence before advice

We separate what the site clearly shows from what still needs analytics or an experiment. A public scan can surface observable risks; it cannot prove why your customers did not buy.

1. Observe

Check accessible pages, purchase actions, mobile blockers, pricing consistency, policy discoverability and subscription transparency.

2. Prioritize

Suppress rules that do not apply to the store type. Surface fewer findings with stronger evidence.

3. Fix or verify

Each finding points to a concrete fix or the next evidence you should collect instead of guessing.
