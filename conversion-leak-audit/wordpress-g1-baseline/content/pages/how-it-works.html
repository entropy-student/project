How it works

The audit checks what can be seen on a limited set of publicly available storefront pages and shows only findings supported by evidence.

  1. Confirm the public website address can be reached safely.
  2. Check a limited number of key storefront pages.
  3. Record what those pages actually show.
  4. Apply trusted checks that fit the pages reviewed.
  5. Show only issues supported by evidence.
  6. If there is not enough evidence, clearly mark the scan as incomplete.

What a public scan cannot know

Traffic quality, actual funnel drop-off, payment decline rates, contribution margin, repeat purchase behavior and causal lift require your own store data or controlled experiments.
