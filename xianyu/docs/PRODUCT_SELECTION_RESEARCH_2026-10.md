## 3. Market demand baseline

### Service market

2025 platform-reported data: 38m annual service buyers, +67.8% YoY; ten major service scenarios including technical development grew orders 96% YoY. 2024 reporting recorded 9.454m side-job/service sellers.

**Reading:** services are a material buyer market, not merely visible seller supply.

### AI / technical / office

2026 H1 platform data:
- 9.816m AI-service orders, +157%
- nearly 5m buyers, +98%
- skill gigs 45.1%
- tutorials/courses 8.1%
- templates/workflows 6.6%
- AI programming/site-building +1732%
- AI PPT/office +264%
- seller average monthly GMV RMB 897

**Reading:** buyer growth is strong, but seller economics are not automatically strong. X2 must find narrow jobs, not choose “AI service” generically.

### Technical drawing

Fresh Xianyu recommendation results show multiple CAD/SolidWorks offers with hundreds to tens of thousands of “想要”, including 44,878 on a RMB 0.50 CAD construction-drawing listing and 4,606 on a RMB 10 SolidWorks listing.

**Reading:** high intent + high competition + severe price compression. Academic/ghostwork demand is excluded; legitimate commercial/redraw/modeling use remains researchable.

### Data / automation

A current Python data/automation offer shows 800 “想要” and 4,153 views, with cleaning, structuring, automation and Excel export in the offer.

**Reading:** the transferable job is “remove repetitive data work”; unauthorized scraping is not the product thesis.

### Low-cost digital assets

A current WordPress template bundle shows 804 “想要” and about 10k views. Generic office-script and digital-resource supply is also abundant.

**Reading:** supply/demand exists, but IP provenance, free substitutes and price compression make generic bundles structurally weak until proven otherwise.

## 10. X2 sampling standard

Per cluster:
1. use 3–5 search phrases;
2. target 20+ current Xianyu listings when public discoverability allows;
3. keep price / 想要 / views / seller-wide sold / listing age separate;
4. collapse same-seller clones and matrices;
5. separate transaction, purchase-intent proxy, attention, and supply density;
6. build price bands + clone density;
7. actively search counterevidence: free AI, RMB 1–5 floor, revision burden, old-listing bias, gray demand, winner concentration;
8. no final score while Payment / Policy / Economics confidence remains U.
