# X2 — Demand Depth & Shortlist — 2026-10-02

Status: PASS  
Scope: Xianyu market research only  
Endpoint: 8-item shortlist, no final winner, no listing, no account/runtime mutation

## 1. Research question

Among the X1 candidates, which offer structures have enough current evidence of real buyer demand on Xianyu to justify expensive E1–E10 research?

This Gate does not ask “what is easiest to make”. It asks:

~~~text
Is there a real buyer job?
→ Is there payment/transaction evidence or a strong platform-level proxy?
→ Can buyers find and understand the offer on Xianyu?
→ Is competition/price compression survivable enough to keep researching?
→ Does free AI erase the value?
→ Is there at least one plausible legal delivery path?
~~~

Supply difficulty can lower later execution priority. It does not erase proven demand.

## 2. Evidence method and limitation

Evidence priority:

1. current official Xianyu rules / official platform docs;
2. Xianyu-reported transaction/buyer data carried by major news outlets;
3. current public Goofish listing pages;
4. documented real seller cases;
5. secondary “side-hustle” articles only as weak discovery/counterevidence.

Important limitation:

Public web indexing does not expose a complete Xianyu search-result ledger. Repeated public-web queries across the 10 X1 demand clusters returned only a sparse subset of current item pages. Therefore X2 does NOT pretend to have a representative 20-item sample for every cluster.

This is handled fail-closed:

- category transaction data may prove a demand pool;
- current direct listings may prove current intent/supply structure;
- “想要” is never converted into orders;
- seller-wide “卖出X件宝贝” is never assigned to one SKU;
- missing SKU transaction/refund/support data remains UNKNOWN;
- final Priority Test is blocked until X3/Minimum Validation resolves the decisive unknowns.

## 3. Current platform facts that change the decision

### 3.1 Services are an explicit platform transaction surface

The current Xianyu Community User Service Agreement (version 2026-05-27, effective 2026-06-15) states that the platform supports item/product/service information and related transactions. It also states that some special service categories require necessary certification before users can publish non-secondhand goods/services, and that publishers must possess lawful rights.

Decision effect:
- service demand is legitimate to research;
- visible listings do not prove our account eligibility;
- rights/IP/data authorization remain hard Gates.

### 3.2 Current platform taxonomy includes the relevant creative-service primitives

The current Goofish homepage publicly exposes Skills categories including:
- 修图
- PPT制作
- 平面设计
- 文案撰写
- 动画设计
- 视频剪辑
- 海报设计
- 建模
- PPT/Office/设计教学

Decision effect:
- PPT/design/video/modeling are not merely off-platform “副业教程” concepts; they are visible platform service taxonomy.

### 3.3 The strongest fresh transaction evidence is AI service demand

Xianyu-reported 2026 H1 data carried by Xinhua:
- 9.816m AI-service orders, +157% YoY;
- nearly 5m buyers, +98%;
- AI skill gigs = 45.1% of AI orders;
- AI tutorials/courses = 8.1%;
- AI templates/workflows = 6.6%, explicitly including workflow customization and AI-agent setup;
- AI programming/site building = +1732%;
- AI PPT/office = +264%;
- technical deployment has the lowest order volume but can reach tens of thousands RMB per order;
- seller average monthly GMV = RMB 897.

Decision effect:
- “AI service” is too broad to be a product;
- result-delivery beats generic tutorial/template as the main research lane;
- high growth coexists with low typical seller GMV, so competition/economics matter.

### 3.4 Broader service demand is not an AI-only phenomenon

2025 platform disclosures reported 38m annual service buyers, +67.8% YoY; ten major scenarios including technical development grew orders 96% YoY.

2024 platform reporting recorded 9.454m side-job/service sellers; software development, graphic design, video editing/production and similar skills were named active categories.

Decision effect:
- technical/creative service demand is structurally broader than one AI hype cycle.

## 4. Price and competition observations

### Data / automation
Current Python data-processing listing:
- display price RMB 4.40; direct buy RMB 8.88;
- 800 “想要”; 4,153 views;
- service description includes extraction, cleaning, structuring, automation and Excel export.

Interpretation:
- direct intent exists;
- very low visible entry pricing creates anchoring pressure;
- custom scope means the displayed price cannot be treated as final project price;
- unauthorized scraping is excluded; user-owned/public-authorized data remains viable.

### CAD / SolidWorks
A current recommendation set contains many offers at RMB 0.50–10 entry prices with “想要” counts from hundreds to tens of thousands, including 44,878, 4,606, 2,945 and 1,214.

Interpretation:
- strongest direct current intent signal among X2 clusters;
- also the clearest price-compression and clone-density warning;
- academic ghostwork is a major contamination risk;
- legitimate commercial redraw/modeling must be isolated before economics can be judged.

### Site building
Current evidence stack:
- platform: AI programming/site-building orders +1732%;
- a current custom APP-development listing uses RMB 10 as display starting price and negotiates scope/price after requirement clarification;
- a current WordPress template bundle is RMB 25.90 with 804 “想要” and about 10k views.

Interpretation:
- demand pool is strongly proven;
- commodity assets and “starting-price” service listings make simple price scraping misleading;
- narrow fixed-scope outcomes are more researchable than generic “开发 APP/网站”.

### Ecommerce image/productivity
Current image-download/productivity tool:
- RMB 2.98–50.98;
- 722 “想要”;
- 5,163 views.

Interpretation:
- ecommerce-image productivity has visible direct intent;
- the sampled tool’s source-access model is not adopted;
- only user-owned/authorized images and original design/standardization survive.

### PPT
Evidence:
- current platform taxonomy explicitly includes PPT制作;
- AI PPT/office orders +264%;
- documented Xianyu seller case: rate grew from RMB 50/page to RMB 200/page; one deck reached RMB 2,500 when the service expanded from visual production toward presentation logic.

Interpretation:
- payment and price ceiling are more credible than generic “卖模板” claims;
- AI lowers basic production cost and therefore increases commoditization;
- a viable offer must sell structure, decision clarity, visual polish and bounded revision rather than “AI生成PPT”.

### Resume / interview
Evidence:
- 2025 platform service reporting explicitly uses “求职面试上闲鱼找专家辅导” as a real use case;
- documented seller case charged RMB 120–130 per resume, later RMB 200–300 per 1,000 Chinese characters, with 2–3 hours of work per resume.

Interpretation:
- willingness to pay exists;
- current labor intensity is structurally high;
- generic rewriting faces severe free-AI substitution;
- rubric-based diagnosis/feedback is more defensible than “代写简历”.

### AI workflow / agent setup
Evidence:
- platform data says templates/workflows are 6.6% of AI-service orders and explicitly includes workflow customization and AI-agent setup;
- technical deployment has low order count but can reach tens of thousands RMB per order.

Interpretation:
- payment reality exists at category level;
- likely higher-ticket and lower-volume than commodity templates;
- support, access authorization and scope control are the core unknowns.

### Video / media work
Evidence:
- current platform taxonomy includes video editing;
- 2024 platform reporting names剪辑制作/摄影摄像 as active side-job directions;
- 2026 AI skill gigs include AI video among paid service categories.

Interpretation:
- demand exists, but X2 lacks strong current SKU-level price/refund data;
- heavy revision burden keeps confidence below PPT/data automation.

## 5. X2 accepted shortlist — 8, NOT ranked

### S1 — Excel / CSV cleaning + office automation
Offer thesis:
User supplies files/data → deterministic clean/merge/dedupe/format/automation → validated workbook/output + clear exception report.

Why it survives:
- direct current intent;
- broader AI office growth;
- outcome can be objectively checked;
- repeatable automation can reduce support cost.

Critical unknown:
Can a fixed-scope offer avoid RMB 1–10 anchoring and stay profitable after requirement clarification and exceptions?

### S2 — Fixed-scope AI programming / light website / micro-tool
Offer thesis:
One bounded business outcome → fixed page/tool/function set → deployed result or source package → explicit exclusions.

Why it survives:
- strongest category growth in current platform data (+1732%);
- technical deployments can command high tickets;
- related site-building assets show current intent.

Critical unknown:
Which narrow sub-job has enough frequency to avoid custom-project scope creep and maintenance burden?

### S3 — PPT structure rewrite + visual polish
Offer thesis:
Client supplies content/goal → logic restructure + visual system → editable deck + bounded revisions.

Why it survives:
- AI PPT/office +264%;
- platform taxonomy is explicit;
- documented real seller pricing proves buyers can pay far above template prices when logic/quality is included.

Critical unknown:
Can proof of structure/decision quality overcome free-AI commoditization without creating revision-heavy bespoke work?

### S4 — AI Workflow / Agent setup for one defined business process
Offer thesis:
One repetitive business process → requirement map → bounded workflow/agent → documentation + acceptance test.

Why it survives:
- explicit platform transaction category;
- lower-volume technical deployment can be high-ticket;
- stronger free-substitute resistance than generic prompt/template sales.

Critical unknown:
Can the offer be standardized enough that access setup, integrations and post-delivery support do not turn each order into consulting?

### S5 — Legitimate commercial CAD / SolidWorks redraw/modeling
Offer thesis:
Client-owned drawing/spec/dimensions → redraw/model/format conversion → editable source + agreed views.

Why it survives:
- exceptionally strong current intent signals;
- clear result artifact.

Critical unknown:
After removing academic ghostwork and ultra-low-price clone listings, is there still enough legitimate commercial demand at a sustainable price?

### S6 — JD-specific resume diagnosis + interview feedback
Offer thesis:
Client supplies JD + resume/background → gap diagnosis/rubric + prioritized edits + optional mock feedback; no fabricated experience.

Why it survives:
- platform explicitly cites job-interview expert demand;
- documented seller payment history proves willingness to pay.

Critical unknown:
Can diagnosis be productized enough to escape 2–3 hours/order and free-AI rewriting?

### S7 — Ecommerce product-image / SKU asset standardization
Offer thesis:
Client-owned images/SKU list → crop/size/background/naming/quality normalization → platform-ready asset package.

Why it survives:
- direct current ecommerce-image productivity intent;
- platform already supports修图/平面设计;
- deliverable is objective and batchable.

Critical unknown:
Is buyers’ willingness to pay for legitimate standardization strong enough once scraping/downloading demand is removed?

### S8 — Video editing / subtitle / structured media cleanup
Offer thesis:
Client-owned raw media → bounded cut/subtitle/cleanup/transcript/chapters → final files with explicit revision limit.

Why it survives:
- platform taxonomy + historical platform-side service evidence + paid AI-video skill category;
- recurring creator/SMB job.

Critical unknown:
Can a narrow package avoid the revision-heavy economics of generic video editing?

## 6. Removed or downgraded after X2

### Personalized travel itinerary — HOLD, removed from shortlist
Evidence found:
- current public recommendation contains a self-made Kansai guide at RMB 26;
- multiple 2025 “副业项目” articles claim AI travel-plan sales, often RMB 9.9–59.9.

Why removed:
- strongest public evidence is low-quality side-hustle marketing rather than platform transaction evidence;
- free AI substitution is unusually strong;
- seasonality and itinerary accuracy/support create risk;
- no strong current SKU payment evidence was found.

### Generic SOP / spreadsheet / template packs — MERGED OR HOLD
Why:
- generic template demand is plausible, but the current platform data already shows templates/workflows as a smaller share than skill delivery;
- generic files have high free-substitute and cloning risk;
- custom operating-sheet work is absorbed into S1/S4 where the mechanism is stronger.

### Generic digital资料 / copied template bundles — KILL
Why:
- rights/IP provenance;
- price compression;
- free substitutes;
- no defensible mechanism.

### Pure transcription / pure OCR — HOLD as standalone
Why:
- free/cheap AI substitutes are strong;
- survives only when bundled into higher-value structured output, S1 or S8.

### Gray growth tools / unauthorized scraping / account sharing / academic ghostwork — KILL
Demand may be strong. That does not make the offer executable.

## 8. What X2 learned about “货源”

The word “货源” hides three different models:

1. reusable digital inventory;
2. self-produced standardized service;
3. partner-delivered professional service.

The demand research currently favors 2 and 3 more than generic inventory resale.

Likely sourcing paths to test in X3:

- S1/S2/S3/S4/S7/S8: SELF_BUILD / AI_ASSISTED_ORIGINAL / OPEN_SOURCE_LICENSED where applicable;
- S5: SELF_SKILL or PARTNER_DELIVERY;
- S6: SELF_BUILD with explicit expertise boundary; no fabricated credentials.

No shortlist item is killed merely because the supplier is not yet chosen.
