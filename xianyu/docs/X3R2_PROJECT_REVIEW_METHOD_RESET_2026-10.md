## 3. Structural problems found

### Problem A — service-first discovery bias

X1’s 50 raw ideas were mostly generated from service jobs:
- website setup/fix;
- Excel cleaning;
- PPT polish;
- CAD/SolidWorks service;
- resume/interview;
- video editing;
- personalized travel planning.

That was reasonable for the former “find a business to test” objective, but it is not a neutral way to map standardized virtual/digital SKUs already traded on Xianyu.

Effect:
- software/AI/Office/productized service ideas are overrepresented;
- native commodity-like virtual goods are underrepresented.

### Problem C — recommendation-page evidence was treated too much like search-market evidence

Many X3R1 SKUs came from “为你推荐” modules attached to a few item pages.

A recommendation module proves:
- the product exists;
- it is currently indexable/recommended;
- the displayed intent count is a real current page field.

It does not prove:
- query-specific search depth;
- independent seller density;
- category market share;
- that multiple recommended items are independent demand sources.

Therefore recommendation-only candidates were sometimes labeled too strongly.

### Problem D — adjacent-demand transfer

Several SKUs are inferred from a nearby market rather than directly observed.

Examples:
- high demand for AI bid-writing software/access was used to support AI bid-writing tutorial + template pack;
- a quotation-management application was used to derive quotation/order/invoice Excel template;
- ecommerce image-download demand was used to derive buyer-owned SKU batch resize/rename tool;
- office macro growth was used to derive several specific Excel/PDF utilities.

These are valid hypotheses, but they are derived candidates, not direct SKU-demand proof.

### Problem E — candidate granularity is inconsistent

Current pool mixes:
- very specific SKU: “vivo X200/X300 Log LUT”;
- product family: “Python Excel/Word/PDF automation script pack”;
- broad system: “Excel inventory + profit + order business system”;
- content niche: “AI manga full workflow tutorial”;
- market behavior: “ecommerce image collection/organization tool.”

Final catalog cannot compare these fairly until normalized.

### Problem F — old opportunity-selection filters still leak into demand research

The installed Xianyu opportunity Skill is built to produce:
- one Priority Test;
- up to two backups;
- policy/economics/delivery/free-substitute checks;
- Minimum Validation.

That is a different endpoint from this project.

For this project:
- free substitutes are useful counterevidence, but do not prove no demand;
- support/economics are out of scope;
- current supplier/right availability is out of scope;
- real listing validation is out of scope;
- operator fit is out of scope.

The Skill remains useful for signal discipline and discovery patterns, not as the project’s final scoring/decision contract.

### Problem G — old S/A/B demand labels were overconfident

The old labels mix:
- direct current listing evidence;
- platform category transaction evidence;
- recommendation-page visibility;
- derived adjacent hypotheses.

They should not sit on one scale without provenance.

## 5. New project-local selection method

The project will no longer use the installed opportunity Skill’s final Priority-Test logic as its endpoint.

### 5.1 Discovery order

Use this order:

~~~text
A. OFFICIAL PLATFORM TAXONOMY
→ what standardized virtual/digital product families Xianyu itself exposes

B. DIRECT CURRENT SKU SCAN
→ concrete live item / searchable product families

C. CATEGORY / PLATFORM TRANSACTION DATA
→ macro evidence only where explicitly tied to the family

D. MULTI-SELLER / MULTI-LISTING REPLICATION
→ independent evidence that the SKU family is not one anomalous listing

E. SERVICE-TO-PRODUCTIZATION / CAPABILITY IDEAS
→ supplemental discovery only, never allowed to outrank direct market evidence by itself
~~~

### 5.2 Candidate provenance classes

Every candidate gets exactly one primary provenance label:

- DIRECT_SKU: directly observed current Xianyu item matching the candidate.
- PLATFORM_CATEGORY: Xianyu official taxonomy exposes the product family.
- PLATFORM_TRANSACTION: platform-reported transaction data explicitly names the product family.
- RELATED_RESULTS_CLUSTER: multiple current related/recommendation items show the family.
- DERIVED_ADJACENT: inferred from another job/product; must not enter final confirmed tier without direct evidence.

### 5.3 Demand evidence levels

Replace old S/A/B with:

#### D4 — DIRECT TRANSACTION / FIRST-PARTY PRODUCT-FAMILY PROOF
Examples:
- platform-reported transaction share/order data specifically naming the family;
- verified SKU-level completed transaction evidence if public and attributable.

#### D3 — REPLICATED DIRECT MARKET EVIDENCE
At least two independent current Xianyu direct SKU/listing signals or a direct SKU plus strong platform/category evidence.

#### D2 — SINGLE DIRECT MARKET EVIDENCE
One current direct SKU/listing or one coherent related-results cluster with measurable intent.

#### D1 — DERIVED / MACRO ONLY
Adjacent-category inference, generic macro growth, or non-Xianyu proxy.

#### U — UNKNOWN
Insufficient evidence.

Important:
- a large “想要” number can strengthen D2, but does not by itself turn D2 into D3/D4;
- platform taxonomy proves a market surface exists, not its sales volume;
- recommendation modules remain RELATED_RESULTS_CLUSTER, not search-share evidence.

### 5.4 Final catalog statuses

- CONFIRMED_DEMAND: D4 or strong D3.
- PROBABLE_DEMAND: D3 / strong D2 with corroboration.
- WATCHLIST: D2 or D1 worth retaining but not sufficiently corroborated.
- MARKET_SIGNAL_ONLY: demand visible, but the observed product form itself is clearly problematic/restricted; kept for market mapping without being presented as a clean executable SKU.

No single winner is required.

### 5.5 Final catalog fields

~~~text
Product type / SKU
Normalized search phrase
Product family
Provenance class
Current price band
Current Xianyu evidence
Independent-signal count
Intent metrics when available
Demand level D4/D3/D2/D1/U
Persistence / seasonality
Counterevidence
Confidence
Status
Source pointers
~~~

No sourcing fields are required.
