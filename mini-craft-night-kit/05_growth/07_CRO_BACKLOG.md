This is a readiness backlog, not an implementation authorization. Each item is grounded in the K4.5 audit. Do not create a CRO experiment until real, trustworthy funnel data identifies a decision-changing issue.

## P0 — Before first qualified traffic (5)

1. Public origin and crawl contract — establish a stable public HTTPS origin; intentionally set indexability, canonical host, robots behavior, and a reachable sitemap. Current site is localhost/noindex with local canonicals and no sampled sitemap endpoint.
2. Production product truth — replace test price/currency, stock, SKU, and unconfirmed product/identifier/image facts with Owner/supplier-approved data that agrees across page, WooCommerce, structured data, checkout, and any later feed.
3. Minimal measurement — select one analytics provider, implement/validate the six-event funnel and UTM contract in a later authorized Gate, and reconcile purchase exactly once to WooCommerce paid/captured state.
4. Privacy, Terms, and consent — obtain Owner/legal review and publish approved public policies; define consent behavior before analytics or marketing-email capture. This is a readiness finding, not legal advice.
5. Contact and transactional delivery — verify the native Contact form reaches its approved destination and required WooCommerce transactional messages are delivered; current delivery is unverified.

## P1 — Before soft launch (7)

1. Search Console — Owner verification on the stable production domain; submit/monitor the production sitemap and URL coverage.
2. Merchant Center/free listings — decide whether to use them; only configure after production price, availability, shipping/returns, public landing page, and feed data agree.
3. Product/Offer structured data — validate existing output and add only substantiated brand, shipping, returns, and identifier data; no invented business facts.
4. Page metadata — prepare relevant titles, descriptions, canonical and Open Graph data plus selective index policy for Home, Shop, Product, FAQ, Shipping, and Contact after origin/model decisions.
5. Images and alt — Owner approves rights and accurate high-resolution product/gallery assets; add descriptive alt only where the image conveys meaningful content.
6. Auxiliary pages and legacy URLs — decide whether About/Blog/Reviews should be public/indexable and define a redirect/retirement policy for old demo product URLs; audit found thin/empty auxiliary pages and legacy URLs returning 404.
7. Email deliverability — choose sender domain/provider and verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC, transactional delivery, suppression/complaint handling, and any consented lifecycle flows.

## P2 — After first real sessions (5)

1. Use Search Console queries and actual landing-page sessions to refine intent mapping and internal links.
2. Review field Core Web Vitals by device and fix the largest observed issue; current local HTML sampling is not field data.
3. Create CRO experiments from observed funnel drop-off with a written hypothesis, one primary metric, guardrail, and stop rule.
4. Request/publish genuine reviews or creator proof only after real customers/partners exist and permissions/consent are established.
5. Compare channel/cohort quality and optimize deliberately selected channels after sufficient observations.

## Defer until data or explicit strategy

- Mass/programmatic AI SEO or broad article production without query/conversion evidence.
- Complex multi-touch/cross-domain attribution and session replay before demonstrated need and consent review.
- Large dashboards, broad experiments, and automation before event definitions and sufficient observations.
- Creator CRM/affiliate automation and cross-border expansion before a real program, approved economics, and operational readiness.
