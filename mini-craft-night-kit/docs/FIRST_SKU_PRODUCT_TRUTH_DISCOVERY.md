## Executive finding

Four concrete Alibaba supplier offers were identified across painting, miniature/book-nook, weaving, and cross-stitch. These are screening leads, not approved SKUs or production truth. All offer facts below are attributed to the listing/platform and remain unverified until confirmed with the supplier and/or a physical sample.

No candidate currently qualifies as launch-ready or has a complete landed cost. None of the four publicly establishes a confirmed US shipping quote, commercial image-use rights, a sufficiently exact kit contents list plus missing-parts remedy, and a supplier-specific defect/return process together. The two richest publicly documented records for bounded follow-up are A (ORFON ND766 paint-by-numbers) and C (Shanghai Yuhan MWK-001 mini loom). This is not a winner selection.

## Evidence labels and method

- VERIFIED: directly observed on the cited public page on the research date. This verifies what the page displayed, not the truth of the seller's claim.
- SUPPLIER_CLAIM: a statement attributed by the listing to its supplier.
- MARKETPLACE_CLAIM: listing, badge, price, rating, protection text, or supplier metadata shown by the marketplace.
- INFERENCE: reasoned product/brand fit, not a proven customer outcome.
- UNKNOWN: not established by the inspected public source.

No supplier was contacted; no account was created; no image was downloaded; no sample/order/payment was made. Alibaba was used as a recognized wholesale marketplace. No independent manufacturer-domain source was located for these exact offer variants in this bounded pass. Listing availability, localized display prices, variants, and terms may change; obtain a dated quote before relying on them.

## Cross-candidate risk and commercial readout

- No listing establishes a delivered US landed cost. Product price alone is not a viable unit-economics input.
- Listing sample claims are not confirmation of actual sample availability, freight, delivery, or suitability. Sample purchase is required to validate usability, consistency, instruction quality, actual contents, product damage/quality, and for B electrical/light safety details.
- Supplier contact is required to confirm USD quote and Incoterm, US ship method/cost and delivery, exact kit BOM/variant, English instructions, customization/private-label rights, commercial use of listing images/art, defect replacement, missing-parts policy, and returns.
- Art/design IP risk applies to all motifs/scenes; no license or authorization was verified. Do not reuse marketplace images in the store or GitHub without written permission.
- D's product page exposes materially less information than A/B/C. B adds a light/electrical compliance path and higher parcel gross weight. C may be an incomplete date-night offer because the listed loom bundle is not demonstrated to be enough for two participants or a polished finished result.
- Do not infer completion duration or date-night success from listing language.
