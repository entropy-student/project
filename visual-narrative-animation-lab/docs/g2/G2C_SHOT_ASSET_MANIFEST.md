# G2C Shot Asset Manifest v0.1

## Purpose
Resolve 18 Director beats into a small reusable asset set before timeline assembly.

## Strategies
- `REUSE`: existing composition/crop
- `VARIANT`: same composition with changed expression, prop, or state
- `NEW_ART`: new major illustration
- `GRAPHIC`: editable card/icon rather than full scene
- `OVERLAY`: downstream text or shape

## Budget
18 beats do not require 18 paintings.

Expected major unique compositions: **8**.
Expected reuse/variant/graphic beats: **10**.

## Text policy
Important readable text is not baked into generated images. Final labels and subtitles are added downstream in Simplified Chinese.

Five-factor labels:
1. 稳定性
2. 思考
3. 输出
4. 效率
5. 反馈
